Button Mash returned with another five tournaments on the 4th of July at Belfast's Accidental Theatre. Let's check out how they went down:

# Invincible VS

(4 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-july-2026/events/invincible-vs/brackets/2310334/3342617/overview))

**FreakingFracker** (Omni-Man/Battle Beast/Conquest) won this closely-fought round-robin tournament with 2-1 wins over second-placed **Pennma** (Thula/Omni-Man/Lucan) and third-placed **Mini_Kai** (Powerplex/Bulletproof/Cecil) and a 2-0 win over **Skeletorr** (Robot/Battle Beast/Cecil).

![INVS: 1) FreakingFracker (Omni-Man/Battle Beast/Conquest) 2) Pennma (Thula/Omni-Man/Lucan) 3) Mini_Kai (Powerplex/Bulletproof/Cecil) 4) Skeletorr (Robot/Battle Beast/Cecil)](/images/uploads/posts/button-mash-july-2026-who-wants-to-be-a-blazbillionaire/button-mash-july-26-top8-invincible.jpg)

# Guilty Gear Strive

(10 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-july-2026/event/guilty-gear-strive/brackets/2289444/3314661)/[VOD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLdghaJ5vhs&t=544s))

**mattie** (Ky) followed up his April win with another GGST tournament victory. Once again, he had to do it from the losers' bracket after losing 3-1 to **Dr K** (Zato) in the winners' semi-final. **FabricatorZayac** (Anji) and **Maka** (Ky) were dealt with before a rematch in the losers' final, which mattie won 3-0, before a pair of 3-1 wins over **Mister_Finer** (Robo-Ky) to retain the Button Mash GGST crown.

![GGST: 1) mattie (Ky) 2) Mister_Finer (Robo-Ky) 3) Dr K (Zato) 4) Maka (Ky) 5) JRS_Russian (Goldlewis)/FabricatorZayac (Anji) 7) Orion (Jam)/MasterEdgelord0 (Nagoriyuki)](/images/uploads/posts/button-mash-july-2026-who-wants-to-be-a-blazbillionaire/button-mash-july-26-top8-strive.jpg)

# Tekken 8

(9 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-july-2026/event/tekken-8/brackets/2289445/3314662)/[VOD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLdghaJ5vhs&t=9342s))

**Hench Man Hoey** (King) claimed a dominant win, not losing a single game on his way. **FreakingFracker** (Victor) took second place despite a loss to **Skeletorr** (Kunimitsu) in winners' bracket, as he got his revenge in a tight losers' final. In the end, though, Hench Man Hoey had too much for him in the grand final.
