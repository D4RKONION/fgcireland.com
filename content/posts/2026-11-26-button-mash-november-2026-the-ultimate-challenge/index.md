27 players attended November's Button Mash event in the Accidental Theatre in Belfast city centre. Here's how it went down:

# Marvel Tokon

(5 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-november-2026/event/marvel-tokon/brackets/2395650/3453600))

**Jo** (Loki/Blade/Black Panther/Magik) took top spot in this round-robin tournament, ahead of **kaworuscott** (Green Goblin/Blade/Spider-Man/Iron Man) and **Pennma** (Ghost Rider/Ms Marvel/Loki/Iron Man).

# Street Fighter 6

(7 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-november-2026/event/street-fighter-6/brackets/2395651/3453601))

The big body domination continued from August's SF6 tournament, thanks to newly-released DLC character Arjun. An Arjun mirror grand final saw **Neilo** come out on top against **Smoke406**, while **kaworuscott** (Marisa) took third and **Yreval** in fourth played Marisa and Mai at different points in the tournament (Guess he went from big body to big bo... nah, too easy).

# Tekken 8

(11 players/[Bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/button-mash-november-2026/event/tekken-8/brackets/2412276/3475775))

After a tightly-contested bracket, the others were left with egg on their faces as **TheEgginator01** (King) claimed the honours with victory over **Skeletorr** (Jun).  **kaworuscott** (Leroy) took third place.
